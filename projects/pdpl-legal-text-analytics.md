# Saudi PDPL Legal Text Analytics

## Overview

This team data-science project analyzed **43 articles** from Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

The project explored Arabic legal text using preprocessing, exploratory analysis, TF-IDF, keyword-frequency analysis, comparative statistics, and LLM-based question-answering experiments.

## My Contribution

My contribution focused on:

- Exploratory data analysis
- Comparative analysis
- Interpretation of statistical and textual patterns
- Communication of results
- Gemini-based Arabic legal Q&A experiments
- Comparison of zero-shot, one-shot, and few-shot prompting strategies

## Analysis

The project examined characteristics such as:

- Article length
- Word and term frequency
- TF-IDF patterns
- Differences between original and amended articles
- Structural variation across legal text

Arabic preprocessing included text normalization and stopword filtering.

## LLM Experiments

I developed Q&A-generation experiments using **Gemini**, comparing:

- Zero-shot prompting
- One-shot prompting
- Few-shot prompting

The experiments explored how prompting strategy affects Arabic legal-text understanding and generated responses.
