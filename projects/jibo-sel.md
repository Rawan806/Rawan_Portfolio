# Jibo Social Robotics Study — KACST–MIT Collaboration

## Overview

This is an ongoing social robotics research study involving **Jibo** and child collaboration.

The study investigates how a social robot may support children's collaborative behavior during structured group activities.

My current work focuses on the behavioral measurement and research-design side of the study rather than robot implementation.

## My Contribution

I am contributing to the study through:

- Literature review on Social and Emotional Learning (SEL)
- Review of behavioral measurement approaches
- Development of structured behavioral observation measures
- Mapping observable behaviors to relevant SEL competencies
- Supporting the design of measurable collaboration indicators

The current behavioral framework focuses on two SEL competency areas:

- **Social Awareness**
- **Relationship Skills**

## Study Context

The planned study compares collaborative activities conducted with and without Jibo support.

Children participate in small-group problem-solving activities while behavioral indicators related to cooperation, turn-taking, communication, and group interaction can be observed.

## Measurement Approach

The measurement design is informed by:

- **CASEL** Social and Emotional Learning framework
- **SSIS SEL** as a reference framework
- A custom structured observation protocol designed for the study context

The custom observation protocol should not be interpreted as a validated SSIS instrument.

## Current Status

The study is ongoing.

My current contribution is focused on literature review, behavioral measurement design, and research methodology. No final experimental results are reported here yet.
